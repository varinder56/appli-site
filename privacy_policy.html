Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

AGC Assist is an unofficial student companion application designed to provide a faster and more convenient mobile experience for accessing academic information.

AGC Assist is an independent application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially operated by any institution.

Information Stored on Your Device

To provide features such as auto-login, offline access, and faster loading times, AGC Assist may store limited information on your device.

  • Roll number or login identifier
  • Login credentials used for sign-in convenience
  • Previously synced academic information such as attendance, profile details, assignments, mentorship information, and fee information
  • Feedback submitted voluntarily by users

Most information used by AGC Assist is stored locally on your device to support app functionality and improve user experience.

How Information Is Used

Information available to the application is used only to:

  • Sign in to your student account
  • Display academic information available through the student portal
  • Provide offline access to previously synced information
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Help investigate issues reported by users

Device Information

When feedback is submitted through the feedback form, limited technical information may be included to help diagnose issues and improve compatibility.

  • Device model
  • Android version
  • App version
  • Screen resolution

Data Storage and Security

AGC Assist uses local device storage to support features such as auto-login, offline access, and faster loading times. Previously synced information may remain available on your device even when an internet connection is unavailable.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their devices and account access. Reasonable efforts are made to handle stored information responsibly.

Disclaimer

Information displayed within AGC Assist is provided for convenience and is based on information available through the student portal.

AGC Assist does not process fee payments, manage academic records, or perform any official institutional functions. The application may redirect users to external services or official portal pages when required.

While efforts are made to present information accurately, users should verify important academic, administrative, or financial information through official institutional systems whenever necessary.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect improvements, new features, or changes in the application.
