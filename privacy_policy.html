Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

Appli is a student utility application designed to help students access academic information more conveniently.

Appli is an independent application and is not affiliated with or officially operated by any institution.

Information We Store

To improve convenience and app functionality, Appli may store limited information locally on your device:

  • Roll number or login identifier
  • Password (securely stored for auto-login)
  • Cached academic data such as attendance, profile, assignments, mentorship, and fee information
  • Feedback submitted voluntarily by users

How Information Is Used

Stored information is used only to:

  • Allow secure sign in
  • Display academic information from the portal
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Help investigate bugs and issues reported by users

Device Information

When feedback is submitted, limited technical information may be attached automatically for debugging purposes, including:

  • Device model
  • Android version
  • App version
  • Screen resolution

Security

Login credentials are stored securely on-device to support features such as auto-login. Academic data may be cached locally to improve loading speed and usability.

Disclaimer

Academic and portal information shown inside Appli is fetched from the institutional portal for convenience purposes.

Although reasonable efforts are made to reflect portal information accurately, discrepancies, temporary errors, delays, or portal-related issues may occur. Users should verify important academic or financial information through official institutional systems when required.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect improvements or changes in the application.

Contact

For feedback or support, please use the feedback option available inside the application.
