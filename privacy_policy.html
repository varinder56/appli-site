Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

Welcome to Appli. Your privacy matters to us. This app is designed to help students access academic information more conveniently.

Important: Appli is an independent student utility application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially operated by the institution.

1. Information We Store

Appli may securely store limited information on your device to provide a smoother experience.

  • Roll number / login identifier
  • Password (stored securely for auto-login)
  • Cached academic information such as attendance, profile, assignments, mentorship, and related data
  • Feedback information voluntarily submitted by users

2. How Your Information Is Used

Your information is used only to:

  • Authenticate login to the student portal
  • Display attendance, assignments, profile, mentorship, fee, and academic information
  • Improve app stability and user experience
  • Provide feedback and debugging support

3. Device Information

When you voluntarily submit feedback, limited technical information may be attached automatically for debugging purposes.

  • Device model
  • Android version
  • App version
  • Screen resolution

4. Data Storage & Security

Sensitive login credentials are stored securely on-device to support auto-login and convenience. Academic information may be cached locally to improve loading performance.

Appli does not sell, rent, or intentionally share personal information with third parties.

5. Accuracy of Information

Appli fetches information from the institutional student portal for convenience purposes. While we strive to reflect portal information accurately, temporary delays, inaccuracies, or discrepancies may occur.

Students should verify important academic or financial information through official institutional systems when required.

6. Network Requests

Appli communicates with the institutional student portal to fetch academic information requested by the user.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to improve transparency and reflect app updates.

8. Contact

For feedback or support regarding Appli, please contact the developer through the feedback section available inside the app.
